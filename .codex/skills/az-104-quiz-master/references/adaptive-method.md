# Adaptive method

## Evidence levels

- `new`: not tested independently
- `developing`: partial knowledge or one correct response
- `secure`: correct explanation plus transfer question
- `durable`: still correct after delayed review
- `repair`: confidently wrong or repeatedly misunderstood

## Selection mix

- 50% weak or due objectives
- 30% objectives related to current projects
- 20% mixed strong-domain retrieval

Keep larger sets close to official weights and avoid answer-letter patterns.

Difficulty runs from Level 1 fact recognition through Level 4 troubleshooting and trade-off analysis. Move up after three independent correct answers with sound reasoning. After two related errors, teach the smallest missing concept and retry with a new scenario.

## Spacing

- Incorrect or guessed: new scenario in-session, then 1 day
- Correct but unsure: 2 to 3 days
- Correct and explained: 7 days
- Durable: 14 to 30 days

Track confidence separately. Diagnose errors as concept knowledge, scope or permissions, service selection, command interpretation, requirement reading, or changed documentation. Never inflate readiness from recently repeated questions.
