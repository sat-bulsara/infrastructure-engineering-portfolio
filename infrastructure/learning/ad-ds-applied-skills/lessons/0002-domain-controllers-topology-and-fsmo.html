Compact model

A domain controller hosts AD DS and participates in multimaster replication. Sites and subnets model network location. Five operations-master roles coordinate the operations that should not be performed independently everywhere.

ScopeFSMO roles
ForestSchema Master, Domain Naming Master
DomainRID Master, PDC Emulator, Infrastructure Master

Worked reasoning

Transfer, do not seize

If the current role holder is online and healthy, transfer the role. Seizure is a recovery action for a failed holder that will not return. Evidence should show ownership before and after.

netdom query fsmo

Get-ADDomainController -Filter * |
    Select-Object HostName, Site, IsGlobalCatalog

Learner task

Complete module 1 and its assessment

After each unit, close the page and write three facts from memory. In your disposable lab, produce a read-only inventory of DCs, FSMO owners, sites, subnets, DNS health, and replication health before changing anything.

Hint 1

Start with discovery. Do not promote or move a DC until the current state and rollback point are recorded.

Hint 2

Your report should answer: what exists, where is it, who owns each role, and is replication healthy?

Mini quiz

Module 1 check

1. Which role is forest-wide?

A. RID Master
B. Schema Master
C. PDC Emulator

2. How many FSMO roles are held per domain?

3. What AD object maps an IP range to a site?

4. The current holder is healthy. Transfer or seize?

5. Which is the best post-change evidence?

A. No error appeared
B. Ownership and AD health queried independently
C. A screenshot of the planned command

Answer explanations
  1. Schema Master is forest-wide.
  2. RID, PDC Emulator, and Infrastructure Master are domain-wide.
  3. An AD subnet maps a network to a site.
  4. Use transfer while the holder is available.
  5. Independent state and health queries prove the change.
