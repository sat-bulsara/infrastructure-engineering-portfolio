Assessment readiness

Close every coverage gap, rehearse with the guide, then complete a closed-guide timed mock.

Outcome

You can decide from evidence whether you are ready for the real lab.

Local mock: 45 minutes

Retrieve first

Brain dump

Two rehearsal modes

  1. Open-guide rehearsal: use the full study guide and record every lookup.
  2. Final local mock: close the guide, Codex, Microsoft Learn, and copied procedures. Use only normal Windows administration tools and built-in command help.

The closed-guide mock is intentionally stricter than an open reference exercise. It reveals whether you can recognise, navigate, verify, and recover under time pressure.

Real assessment rule

No Codex or other AI assistance

Microsoft's candidate agreement prohibits AI assistance during the real assessment. Do not copy, photograph, publish, or send assessment content. Follow the instructions displayed in the lab about permitted tools and methods.

Learner task

Open the gate

Complete every Independent cell in the coverage checklist with real evidence. Repeat anything that used full steps. Then prepare a clean snapshot and open the timed mock.

Hint 1

A weak area is useful information, not failure. Practise only that operation with changed names and values.

Hint 2

Do not repeat one-way configuration changes on the same environment. Restore a snapshot or practise verification and safe use.
